# Arun Traders – Milk Collection & Society Management System

> **Supported by VK Milk**  
> **அருண் டிரேடர்ஸ் • பால் சேகரிப்பு மையம் • VKA Milk ஆதரவுடன்**

A modern, professional, responsive web application for a rural Indian milk collection society.

---

## 🌟 Key Features Implemented

1. **Modern Login Page with Role-Based Access**
   - Roles: **Admin / Society Owner**, **Collection Operator**, **Farmer**.
   - Bilingual branding (English & Tamil).
   - Quick demo login buttons for instant testing.

2. **Powerful Dashboard & Data Visualizations**
   - KPI Cards: Total Farmers (126), Active Today (94), Today's Total Milk (1,284 L), Morning/Evening breakdown, Average FAT (4.2%), Average SNF (8.5%), Today's Estimated Amount (₹82,450).
   - Interactive Charts: Morning vs Evening Collection, 7-Day Quality Trends (FAT/SNF).
   - Real-time "Today's Collection" table with search & filters.

3. **Farmer Management & Detailed Profiles**
   - Add, Edit, Delete, Search, Filter active/inactive farmers.
   - Detailed Profile View: Total milk supplied, average daily milk, average FAT/SNF, earnings, pending payments, and collection history.
   - **20 realistic demo farmers** (Ramesh Kumar, Suresh, Arun Kumar, Meena, Lakshmi, Selvi, Kumar, Murugan, Priya, Saravanan, etc.).

4. **Fast Milk-Entry Interface (Collection Operator)**
   - Quick input for Farmer, Shift (Morning/Evening), Milk Type (Cow/Buffalo/Mixed), Quantity in Litres, FAT %, SNF %, CLR.
   - **Database-driven rate calculation** (`Amount = Litres × Rate`).
   - Estimated nutritional value label ("Estimated nutritional value: ~68 kcal / 100ml based on FAT & SNF").
   - Digital receipt generation with **Print Receipt**, **Download PDF**, and **Share**.

5. **Dedicated Morning & Evening Collection Pages**
   - Shift-wise summaries and searchable tables.

6. **Rate Chart Management (Admin)**
   - Configurable rate table stored in database state (Cow, Buffalo, Mixed milk slabs for FAT/SNF).

7. **Farmer Financial Ledger & Payment Management**
   - Opening balance, milk earnings, bonuses, deductions, advances, payments, closing balance.
   - Payment tracking (Pending, Paid, Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer).

8. **Professional Reports & Advanced Analytics**
   - Daily, Morning, Evening, Farmer-wise, Quality, and Payment reports with export and print support.
   - Intelligent Society Insights ("Evening collection increased by 12% compared with last week.").

9. **Farmer Portal**
   - Individual farmer dashboard showing today's milk, FAT, SNF, amount, monthly summaries, and history.

10. **System Settings**
    - Society name, address, phone number, VKA Milk branding, collection times, and rate chart parameters.
